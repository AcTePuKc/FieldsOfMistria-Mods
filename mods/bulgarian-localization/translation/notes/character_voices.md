# Character voice notes

Working notes for preserving character voice and relationships during the Bulgarian localization.

These notes are translation guidance, not canonical lore. Prefer the source dialogue and in-game context when they conflict with a shorthand description here.

## Eiland / Ейланд

- Educated, earnest, historically minded and enthusiastic about archaeology and Mistria's past.
- Often formal or bookish, but should still sound natural in Bulgarian rather than academic for its own sake.
- Can become visibly excited about ruins, artifacts, history and research.
- Warm and well-meaning; avoid making him stiff or pompous unless the source explicitly does so.
- Watch for archaeology/history terminology and canonical names of artifacts, places and eras.

## Errol / Ерол

- Older, warm and jovial; former miner who became a museum curator.
- Strong interest in local history, mining and the museum.
- Paternal/avuncular tone works well; friendly rather than overly formal.
- Can sound nostalgic, but not frail or gloomy by default.
- Prefer familiar, idiomatic Bulgarian over literal English turns of phrase.

## Elsie / Елси

- Worldly, playful, dramatic, flirty and shameless in the best sense.
- Loves gossip, teasing and innuendo; do not sanitize jokes or romantic implications.
- Confident and socially experienced; she should sound lively, not prudish or grandmotherly.
- Uses "Juni" for Juniper -> `Джуни`.
- Ordinary dialogue generally uses `леля Елси`; exact genealogy can use the precise kinship term when needed.

## Hemlock / Хемлок

- Warm, grounded innkeeper with a strong family-and-hearth personality.
- Former musician; still casual and jovial, and can joke about his old band life.
- Bartends, brews and plays lute.
- Very fond of Josephine and generally fatherly toward others.
- Hospitality should sound sincere and relaxed, not ceremonious.
- Josephine nickname `Josie` -> `Джози`; source-specific `Jo` -> `Джо`.

## Josephine / Жозефин

- Warm, maternal innkeeper; confident, lightly teasing and very comfortable taking care of people.
- Loves Hemlock, food, hospitality and friendly competition, including drinking jokes.
- Can fuss over people without sounding saccharine.
- Keep her warmth direct and practical rather than overly sentimental.
- Source-specific `Jo` -> `Джо`.

## Juniper / Джунипър

- Dry, haughty, sardonic and extremely self-assured.
- Vain in a funny, charismatic way; often treats her own superiority as an objective fact.
- Witchy/alchemical vocabulary, but dialogue should still sound conversational rather than pseudo-archaic.
- Mildly dismissive of village life and especially of having to work, while clearly being attached to people more than she admits.
- Likes money, wine, shoes, baths, her appearance and her crystal ball.
- Preserve vanity, innuendo and jokes instead of softening them.
- More biting and self-confident than Josephine.
- `Въх...` can suit theatrical/self-important moping where the source supports it, but should not be overused.
- Crystal-ball jokes often deliberately resemble modern screen/TV/doomscrolling habits; preserve the joke without modernizing beyond the source.
- When she compares herself with the Witch Queens, keep the ambition and ego intact.
- Direct-address plural (`<they>`) must use Bulgarian plural agreement consistently.
